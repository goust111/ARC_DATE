## 2. 拓扑配对与标记供色连线

- tags:
  - operation:连线, draw_line
  - operation:计数, count
  - object:封闭区域, enclosed_region
  - object:标记像素, marker_pixel
  - relation:孔数配对, hole_count_pairing
  - condition:交叉按孔数优先, crossing_by_hole_count
  - condition:忽略颜色诱饵, color_decoy
- 参考题目题号: 9 (0a2355a6), 20 (40853293), 6 (444801d8)

### docstring

```text
把孔洞数相同的两个闭合对象用线段连接，线色取自其中一方孔洞内的标记像素。

输入结构:
- 黑色背景上有偶数个多色闭合前景对象，每个对象包围一到若干个 4 连通黑色孔洞。
- 每个孔洞数恰好由两个对象共享；同对的两个对象的包围盒中心恰好共行或共列。
- 每一对中恰有一个对象的某个孔洞内含一枚单像素彩色标记。
- 对象自身颜色互不约束：可能出现颜色相同但孔洞数不同、互不配对的干扰对象。

规则:
- 按孔洞数将对象两两配对，对象颜色与配对无关。
- 沿共享的行或列，在两对象轮廓之间的背景上画一条一格宽线段，线色为该对标记像素的颜色。
- 两条线交叉处保留孔洞数较大那一对的线色；标记像素与所有对象原样保留。

关键洞察:
- 配对键是拓扑孔数而非表面颜色——同色对象可能毫不相干；标记像素不参与配对，
  只负责给线供色，交叉裁决也由拓扑量而非颜色决定。
```

    colors: each cell is non-background; 2-8 unique colors per ring; duplicates allowed in corners and sides
    separation: bounding boxes of any two rings must not overlap or be 4-adjacent (min 1 cell gap on at least one axis)
    placement: may touch grid border; top-left corner anywhere in [0..rows-3] x [0..cols-3]
  transform:
    corners (TL,TR,BR,BL): cycle values CCW by 1 step (TL<-TR<-BR<-BL<-TL)
    sides (T,R,B,L): cycle values CW by 1 step (T->R->B->L->T)
    background unchanged

generation_strategy:
  approach: direct input construction (task is simple transform-in-place)
  steps:
    1. Pick grid size (7x7 [observed], or allow 6-10 for variety) and background color
    2. Choose ring count 1-2; place each ring's top-left corner ensuring 3x3 fits in grid and no two rings' 3x3 bboxes overlap or are 4-adjacent (gap >= 1 on at least one axis)
    3. For each ring, fill 8 surround cells with random non-background colors (allow repeats); set center to background
    4. Fill remaining cells with background
    5. Compute output by applying the corner-CCW / side-CW rotation to each ring
