### docstring

```text
把每个只画了一半的查询形状沿其轴标记镜像补全，再到图例中按形状等价查色并整形重绘。

输入结构
- 一条单色分隔线把网格分为图例区与查询区；图例区内是若干互不同色的完整单色精灵。
- 查询区内所有形状共用一种占位色，且每个都只是某完整形状的一半，旁边紧贴一条短黄色线段标出镜像轴。
- 以半形直接去比对图例，任何一个都对不上。

规则
- 将每个查询半形沿其黄色轴线镜像，得到补全后的完整形状。
- 将补全形状与图例精灵在旋转与反射等价意义下匹配，取得匹配精灵的颜色。
- 在原位绘出完整形状并整体涂成匹配色，同时抹去轴标记；图例与分隔线保持不变。

关键洞察
- 真正参与查询的对象从未被完整画出，图例检索只有在镜像补全之后才能成功；黄色线段不是装饰而是生成元的对称轴。
```

    count: 1-4 equal-arm plus signs, all sharing one radius
    radius: 2-3 (arm length from center)
    full_size: 4*radius+1 cells per unclipped plus
    placement: all plus cells must be non-black; at most 1 plus may be clipped by grid edge [observed]
    separation: pluses must not overlap (manhattan distance between centers > 2*radius)
    red_markers: each plus has 3 to 4*radius+1 of its cells colored red (rest are grey); red fraction 0.33-0.78
    arm_constraint: each plus has >=1 red cell in its horizontal arm AND >=1 in its vertical arm
    radius_signal: at least one plus must have a full-diameter red span (2*radius+1) along one row or column within an unbroken non-black run, so the radius can be inferred
    coverage: every red cell in the grid belongs to exactly one plus
  transform: recolor grey cells within each identified plus to teal; red and black unchanged

generation_strategy:
  approach: output-first
  steps:
    1. Choose grid dimensions (11-20 x 12-22), radius (2-3), and plus count (1-4).
    2. Fill the entire grid with random black/grey (~50% each, per-cell coin flip).
    3. Place plus centers ensuring no overlap (manhattan dist > 2*radius) and sufficient in-bounds room.
    4. For each plus, force all its cells to grey (overwrite any black).
    5. For each plus, randomly choose 3 to 4*radius+1 cells to mark red (ensuring >=1 red in H arm and >=1 in V arm). Ensure at least one plus has a full-diameter red span on its row or column.
    6. Verify no accidental red-span ambiguity (no non-black run outside pluses produces an odd span >= 2*radius+1 with >=2 red cells). If violated, re-randomize the offending area.
    7. Input = grid from step 5. Output = copy with each plus's non-red (grey) cells recolored teal.
