### docstring

```text
复原相互遮挡的实心矩形，按由前到后的层序排列成柱状图，柱高等于每个矩形被遮住的像素数。

输入结构：
- 黑色背景上有若干个颜色互不相同的实心矩形，它们相互重叠成一摞，每个矩形只露出一部分。
- 每个矩形至少露出两个对角的角点，因此其完整范围可以复原。
- 任意两个重叠矩形之间可见的覆盖关系构成一条完整的前后链。

规则：
- 由可见角点复原每个矩形的完整范围。
- 若矩形 A 的完整范围内某格显示的是矩形 B 的颜色，则 B 在 A 之前；由此排出全部矩形的前后总序。
- 统计每个矩形被其它矩形遮住的格数。
- 输出宽度等于矩形个数、高度等于最大被遮格数的黑底网格：第 k 列对应前后序中第 k 个矩形，
  用其颜色从顶部向下涂其被遮格数那么多格。

关键洞察：
- 柱状图的排列顺序不是按数量排序，而是按遮挡推出的深度；柱子的高度数的恰恰是画面上
  看不见的那部分面积，最前面的矩形对应一根空柱。
```
</new_puzzle>

```yaml
      rect_col acts as wildcard (matches any color)
    interior: each piece has 1-2 non-rect_col connected patterns using interior_cols
    piece_fill: every piece contains non-rect_col pixels; 15-55% of piece area is non-rect_col
    scattered: pieces placed at random non-overlapping positions on bg, separated by >= 1 bg cell [observed]
  anchor_piece: exactly one piece contains the marker's missing cell at its matching corner
  assembled_rectangle:
    area equals sum of all piece areas; all four outer edges are entirely rect_col
    interior_patterns: irregular shapes varying widely (rectangles, frames, L-shapes, amorphous blobs, etc.), each with one of interior_cols
    interior_fill: 35-85% of interior (inside outer 1-pixel rect_col border) is interior_cols; rest is rect_col
    color_separation: different interior_cols are 8-disconnected (separated by rect_col in all 8 directions)
    one corner is pinned to the marker's missing cell
    output grid = input grid size, bg everywhere except the assembled rectangle

generation_strategy:
  approach: output-first, then scatter
  steps:
    - pick grid size, bg, rect_col, marker_col, 1-3 interior_cols (all distinct)
    - pick a corner type (TL/TR/BL/BR) and choose a marker position with room for the rectangle
    - build the assembled rectangle R whose four outer edges are all rect_col;
      fill interior with 1-3 irregular connected patterns of interior_cols satisfying interior_patterns,
      interior_fill and color_separation constraints, then tight-crop to pattern bbox + 1-pixel rect_col border
    - partition R into rectangular pieces that tile it exactly; ensure matching_edges,
      rect_col_edges,   es, and piece_fill constraints are all satisfied
    - designate the corner piece as the anchor; place the 3-pixel L-marker adjacent to
      its corner on the grid (opposite side from the rectangle interior)
    - scatter all pieces to random non-overlapping positions on the bg grid, preserving
      each piece's pixel content (no rotation); ensure >= 1 bg cell gap between pieces
    - output grid: place the assembled rectangle at the marker corner, fill rest with bg
```
</generation_spec>
</reference_puzzle>
