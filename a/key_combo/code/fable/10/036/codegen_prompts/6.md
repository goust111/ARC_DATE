## 6. 角标色序定层序

- 参考题目题号: 10 (`272f95fa`)、5 (`281123b4`)、4 (`6a11f6da`)

### docstring

```text
九宫分区中十字位的五个图案面板，按四角单点的顺时针色序自顶向底确定图层顺序后叠加成单区域输出。

输入结构：
- 两横两纵分隔线把网格切成 3x3 等尺寸区域。
- 上、下、左、右、中五个区域各含一种独有前景色的图案，背景视为透明。
- 四个角区域各恰含一个单像素色点，四点颜色是五种面板色中的四种。

规则：
- 从左上角起顺时针读四角点色，依次作为第 1 至第 4 层，第 1 层最上。
- 五种面板色中未被角点点名的那一种作为第 5 层（最底层）。
- 自底向顶把对应面板的非背景像素依次叠绘到单区域尺寸的输出画布上。

关键洞察：
- 角区域不是图层而是图例；层序藏在角点的环形阅读顺序里，
  而缺席恰恰是信息——没被点名的颜色沉在最底层。
```

```yaml
    radiate: fill continues outward through the opening, bounded by rays from endpoints
      until grid edge. Ray slope matches arm-angle mode: 1:1 arms → 45° diagonal rays,
      1:2 arms → 1:2 slope rays (alternating diagonal and depth-only steps)
    shadows: obstacles in the fill zone block fill propagation along radiate_dir behind them
    preserved: wall, fill seed, all obstacle pixels remain unchanged

generation_strategy: |
  Output-first: build the wall + fill output, then strip the fill to get the input.
  1. Pick grid size, 3-5 colors {bg, wall, fill, obstacle(s)}, and radiate_dir.
  2. Build the wall: place it so the closed end has >=2 margin from its edge and the
     opening has >=3 cells toward the radiate edge. Pick arm-angle mode (1:1 or 1:2,
     same for both arms). Draw two arms as zigzag chains from the closed end outward:
     1:1 mode uses diagonal steps; 1:2 mode strictly alternates depth-only and diagonal
     steps (evenly spaced at rows 2,4,6,...). Optionally add short bars at turns. Verify single 8-connected component, exactly 2 endpoints,
     17-25 pixels, Chebyshev endpoint gap 5-7.
  3. Place fill seed: pick a bg cell inside the enclosure near the closed end,
     8-adjacent to a wall pixel. Optionally grow to a small blob (size 1-6).
  4. Place 0-5 obstacle pixels on bg cells (0-2 distinct colors, singletons).
  5. Run the solver to produce the output; reject if <20 newly-filled cells or
     fill does not reach at least one grid border.
  6. The constructed grid (before running solver) is the input; solver output is the output.
```
</generation_spec>
</reference_puzzle>
