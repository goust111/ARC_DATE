<description>
Align a central rectangle to two colored frame-edge controls via rotation/reflection.

Input structure:
- bg grid with two solid-colored border edges (distinct non-bg colors).
- A central filled rectangle with 1px colored guide edges on two sides, colors matching the frame controls.

Rule:
- Rotate/mirror the object so its guide edges face the same sides as the matching frame controls.
- Crop off the 1px guide edges from aligned sides.

Key insight: Guide-edge colors encode target frame sides; the correct dihedral transform matches both simultaneously.
</description>

spec:
  grid: 22-29 rows x 22-29 cols, square, bg=0
  frame:
    control_sides: 2 adjacent sides fully filled with distinct non-bg colors (ctrl_A, ctrl_B)
    noise_sides: the opposite 2 sides have sparse random color-5 cells (~35-60% density) mixed with bg
    corner_rule: shared corner cell of 2 control sides = one of the two ctrl colors; shared corner of 2 noise sides = bg or noise
  object:
    placement: single filled rectangle floating in the interior (not touching any border)
    fill: dominant color, distinct from bg, ctrl_A, ctrl_B, and 5
    guide_edges: exactly 2 adjacent sides are 1px solid guide-color strips; guide colors == {ctrl_A, ctrl_B}
    guide_corner: the corner where the 2 guide edges meet is bg(0); guide corner != control corner
    core: rectangle remaining after stripping guide edges; 5x5 to 20x20 [observed]
    pattern: 2-5 non-fill colors forming sparse motifs inside core; fill covers >=60% of core; may include bg(0) cells as part of pattern
  colors: 7-9 distinct colors total; bg=0, noise=5 always, 2 ctrl/guide colors, 1 fill, 2-5 pattern colors [observed]
  output: core after the dihedral transform that aligns guide-edge colors to matching frame control sides, then guide edges cropped off

generation_strategy:
  approach: output-first
  steps:
    1. Pick 2 adjacent control sides (one of 4 corner configs); assign 2 distinct ctrl colors (not 0, not 5).
    2. Build the core rectangle (5-20 rows x 5-20 cols) filled with fill color; scatter 2-5 pattern colors as sparse motifs (fill >=60%).
    3. Pick the dihedral transform T that will map guide edges to control sides. Attach 1px guide edges (colors ctrl_A, ctrl_B) on 2 adjacent sides of the core; set their shared corner to bg(0). This forms the full object.
    4. Apply the INVERSE of T to the object to get the input-state object (guide edges now on different sides from controls).
    5. Build the grid: fill bg=0, paint control sides solid, paint noise sides with ~40-55% random color-5, place the inverse-transformed object in the interior (not touching borders).
