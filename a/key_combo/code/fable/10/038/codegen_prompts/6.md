### docstring

```text
一句话总述：按端帽颜色串成的隐藏链条决定装填次序，把各条纹按自身本体长度的格数依次连续装填进灰色正方形。

输入结构：
- 网格上有一个 k×k 的灰色正方形和若干条一格宽、互不同色的条纹，条纹方向一致（全横或全竖），不同条纹的本体长度可以相同。
- 每条条纹的一端额外附着一个端帽像素（颜色不同于本体）：端帽颜色等于链条中下一条条纹的本体颜色；恰有一条条纹没有端帽，它是链条的末尾；链条的开头是本体颜色未被任何端帽引用的那条条纹。
- 所有条纹本体长度之和恰等于 k²；条纹的空间位置、长度大小均与链条次序无关。

规则：
- 以灰色正方形为输出画布，输出仅为该正方形大小。
- 条纹全横时按行优先（自左而右、自上而下）、全竖时按列优先（自上而下、自左而右）连续填格。
- 从链头条纹开始，沿端帽指针逐条处理：每条颜色连续填入与其本体长度相等的格数（不含端帽），颜色块允许跨行（或跨列）折行，直至填满整个正方形。

关键洞察：
- 装填次序既不是空间顺序也不是长度顺序（二者都是诱饵，且长度可能并列而根本排不出全序）：每条条纹用端帽颜色指名"下一棒"，必须先在图中解出这条隐藏的接力链，再做面积守恒的折行连续装填，颜色边界一般不落在行尾。
```

```yaml
    single connected component of main_color; does NOT touch the edge-line row/col
    amorphous blob shape (may have bg holes); 10-51 pixels, ~15-32% of grid area
    main_color pixel count > marker_color pixel count
  markers:
    scattered single pixels of marker_color; total 4-22
    embedded (>=3 main_color 4-neighbors): 1-7, sit inside the main object
    isolated (0 main_color 4-neighbors): 3-15, sit on bg away from main object
    each isolated marker's cell 1 step toward edge must be in-bounds and bg
    no two isolated markers' extension targets collide
  output_rule:
    embedded markers recolored to main_color (defect repair)
    isolated markers kept + paint adjacent bg cell in edge direction with marker_color
    all other pixels unchanged; output same size as input

generation_strategy:
  approach: output-first
  steps:
    1. Pick grid size (8-13, square), choose 4 distinct colors for bg/edge/main/marker.
    2. Pick one border (top/bottom/left/right) and fill that row/col with edge_color.
    3. Place an amorphous main-object blob (single connected component, 15-30% of non-edge area)
       at least 2 rows/cols away from the edge line; grow via random walk or DLA.
    4. Choose 1-7 interior cells of the blob that have >=3 main_color 4-neighbors;
       these become embedded marker sites. Recolor them to marker_color to get the input;
       in the output they stay main_color.
    5. Scatter 3-15 isolated markers on bg cells not 4-adjacent to main_color.
       For each, verify the cell 1 step toward the edge is in-bounds, bg, and not
       already used by another marker or extension. In the output, paint that
       extension cell with marker_color as well.
    6. Input = output with embedded markers flipped to marker_color and extension
       cells reverted to bg.
```
</generation_spec>
</reference_puzzle>

2. `generate_input()` 必须随机生成多样样例，不能固定死。随机维度应覆盖：网格大小、对象数量、位置、颜色、方向、形状参数或关键分支。注意若描述中存在若干，需实现而不是只有单个，包括object或者对应关系等等。且若涉及到最短路径，则输入网格中必涉及到多条路径，对选择造成干扰。
